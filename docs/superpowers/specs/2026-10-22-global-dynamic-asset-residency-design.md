## Intent and decision

Remove the **artificial global fixed decoded-byte ceiling** for the shared game asset cache and scene-owned residency, not just for P2 or a particular character. Preserve scene-scoped demand/loading, required-asset completeness, critical portrait/Idle priority, optional effect demand, the two concurrent image transports, fallback behavior, texture teardown, LRU/trim of inactive entries and observable high-water diagnostics. This does **not** imply unlimited physical browser/GPU memory or guarantee that every phone can decode/upload any future roster. Keep existing per-file 4 MiB encoded, 2048-pixel dimension and 4194304-pixel guards and verified manifest metadata; actual image/texture allocation failure must be reported and must not silently mark the scene ready.

The preceding `2026-10-22-p2-awakening-capacity-design.md` 11-piece Chiru KO direction is **superseded, not implemented**. Preserve original Chiru KO pixels and runtime derivative. The historical 45-key/16611224 decoded and proposed 49-key/17756496 figures describe the former 16 MiB policy only; neither proves device RAM use or a new capacity PASS. No character-specific exception, reduced art density, dropped required asset, artificial per-character budget, or new replacement hard cap.

## Components and contract

1. `src/assets/schema.js` retains image/manifest safety guards, but no longer exports a fixed total decoded-byte maximum as a runtime acceptance gate. A tunable *soft reclamation target* can remain for unpinned/inactive cache entries, never as a rejection threshold for required content. Entry-count behavior must not reject a legal required encounter merely because its total count crosses the former 64; bound **in-flight concurrency** independently, and reclaim unpinned entries to avoid unbounded inactive retention.
2. `src/runtime/assetCache.js` is a shared transport/decode cache, not authoritative scene ownership. It validates the individual loaded record before caching, deduplicates pending loads, keeps the existing two-request concurrency, and may trim inactive entries. A cache miss/eviction never invalidates an already registered scene texture. A per-asset decoded size above a former `maxBytes` must not be mistaken for a metadata mismatch. Cache transport/fallback failures remain visible to the scene-required path, rather than resolving to a procedural record and declaring required artwork ready.
3. `src/runtime/assetResidency.js` owns registered textures per scene, with encounter pins for **all** required keys and stage/optional loads only on demand. Remove the checks against `hardBytes`, `softBytes` as a hard boundary, former 64 entries and the 131072-byte reserve as a gate; no reserve is necessary against a removed ceiling. Keep accurate live/reserved/high-water measurements, deduplication, LRU reclamation of unpinned optional textures, closed-scene/late-load safety, failed GPU registration cleanup and deterministic destroy. Required load failures reject `prepare` and keep `ready=false`; optional failures remain nonfatal and observable. Native allocation failure is an actual failure, not permission to render a partial encounter.
4. `src/runtime/assetPresenter.js` keeps critical portrait/Idle ordering and the full required encounter key list, never relabeling missing Move/KO/actions as optional. Stage asset remains optional on demand. Scene teardown removes its textures and releases its pins; cache trim can release stale CPU references on scene transition. Diagnostics distinguish decoded estimates for cache/scene and reservations from actual device memory, which is not measured here.
5. Awakening uses this **generic** policy after separately reviewed observational cast/action/damage/status contract and faithful source-exact derivative installation. It must not introduce Chiru-specific packing, inflate actor scale or damage geometry, invent victim impacts/protection, alter gameplay clocks or re-open accepted art. The approved review GIF is not live capture. The existing preflight's fixed-cap blocker becomes historical; its event/status findings remain review candidates.

## Alternatives considered

- **Selected:** one shared demand/residency system with no fixed decoded-byte total gate, full required readiness and inactive reclamation. It directly matches the player's whole-game policy and keeps real load/allocation failures explicit.
- Retain 16 MiB and losslessly repack Chiru KO: can fit one projected encounter under the former policy but creates a character-specific capacity workaround; expressly superseded.
- Raise the fixed ceiling (e.g. 32 MiB) or make each character optional: does not implement the requested dynamic policy and risks a new silent missing-art condition; rejected.
