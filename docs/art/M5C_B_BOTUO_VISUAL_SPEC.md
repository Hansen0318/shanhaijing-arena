# M5C-B Batch1 Visual Spec — 猼訑

## Status
**CONCEPT DIRECTION — PLAYER REVIEW PENDING**

## 1. Role
Type: Power
Role: Tank
Combat identity: durable front guard / protector.

## 2. Body plan
Humanoid / anthropomorphic biped. Broad shoulders, heavy center of gravity, stable stance. Sheep/goat-like head treatment, curved horns, dense fur accents, hoof-influenced lower legs permitted.

## 3. Creature identity
Preserve:
- sheep/goat-like head;
- strong curved horns;
- dense fur around neck/shoulders;
- heavy, steadfast silhouette.

Avoid:
- literal quadruped;
- generic human knight with token horns;
- overly slim assassin proportions.

## 4. Equipment language
Heavy gauntlets, forearm guards, shoulder armor, shield-like bracer or impact mace are valid. Equipment may be invented and should communicate protection/impact rather than speed.

## 5. Color direction
Stone, dark brown, iron, muted gold, with restrained Power accent. High contrast horn/fur silhouette.

## 6. portraitSquare
Head + upper torso, 3/4 angle. Horn silhouette must remain readable at small size. Expression calm/unyielding.

## 7. collectionArt
Frontline guarding pose, one arm/bracer forward, weight planted. Show protector identity rather than berserker aggression.

## 8. battleIdle
Humanoid guarded stance; knees slightly bent; shoulders squared; weapon/bracer ready.

## 9. idle micro-animation
4-frame lightweight loop:
- slow chest/shoulder breathing;
- slight horn/head settle;
- small armor/fur secondary motion;
- subtle weight shift;
- no locomotion.

## 10. Batch2 direction
Hit: short recoil but recovers quickly.
KO: heavy grounded collapse/brace.
Cast: planted guard/impact wind-up.

## 11. Acceptance
Must read as sturdy Tank, retain sheep/goat mythic identity, and fit shared humanoid animation vocabulary.
