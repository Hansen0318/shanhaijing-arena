# M5C-B Batch1 Visual Spec — 狌狌

## Status
**CONCEPT DIRECTION — PLAYER REVIEW PENDING**

## 1. Role
Type: Power
Role: Attacker
Combat identity: aggressive bruiser / chase specialist.

## 2. Body plan
Humanoid / anthropomorphic biped, muscular but mobile. Ape/primate-derived head, forearms, posture and fur cues may be emphasized while keeping shared humanoid animation compatibility.

## 3. Creature identity
Preserve:
- primate/humanlike beast cues;
- strong upper-body/forearm silhouette;
- rugged southern-mountain identity;
- relentless close-range feel.

Avoid:
- literal quadruped ape;
- oversized gorilla proportions that break common rig framing;
- full heavy tank silhouette that overlaps 猼訑.

## 4. Equipment language
Heavy gauntlets, knuckle weapons, chain/impact weapon, shoulder guards, or chase-oriented grappling/impact gear are valid. Power should read through impact, not immobility.

## 5. Color direction
Deep brown/charcoal, dark red, bronze, muted ochre. Strong forearm/weapon contrast.

## 6. portraitSquare
Head + upper torso, strong brow/jaw/fur cues, aggressive but not monstrous.

## 7. collectionArt
Forward-driving stance, one arm leading, legs positioned for chase. Emphasize sustained melee pressure.

## 8. battleIdle
Humanoid bruiser stance; hands/gauntlets forward, center of gravity ready to spring.

## 9. idle micro-animation
4-frame loop:
- breathing through shoulders;
- small hand/gauntlet flex;
- slight torso rock;
- fur/strap motion;
- no walking.

## 10. Batch2 direction
Hit: short forceful recoil.
KO: heavy collapse.
Cast: brief crouch/coil before chase or slam.

## 11. Acceptance
Must read as Power Attacker, distinct from 猼訑 Tank, and remain compatible with shared humanoid combat animation language.
