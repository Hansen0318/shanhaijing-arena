# M5C-B Batch1 Visual Spec — 赤鱬

## Status
**CURRENT NEXT GATE — CONCEPT DIRECTION / PLAYER REVIEW PENDING**

This is the next Chapter1 character after accepted 鹿蜀 and 猼訑 static-runtime presentation. Only the Concept gate is open. Do not produce portraitSquare, collectionArt, battleIdle, locomotion frames, attack/cast animation or VFX until the concept is explicitly approved.

## 1. Gameplay identity
- Character: 赤鱬
- Type: **Blast**
- Role: **Support**
- Combat identity: ranged healer / rear support.
- Read: calm aquatic support who projects water/healing effects from behind the frontline.

## 2. Body plan and locomotion identity
Humanoid / anthropomorphic aquatic body, slim-to-medium build, readable arms/hands for support casting.

**Locomotion classification: Aquatic Hover Humanoid.**
- May remain visibly suspended above the ground in Arena.
- Does not need a conventional walking cycle.
- Future movement animation should use gentle forward/back body drift, fin/tail/cloth follow-through and water-linked buoyancy cues.
- No actor-coordinate bob that changes gameplay position; hover is presentation-only around the authoritative actor position.
- Do not use a mermaid tail that destroys the shared humanoid combat/action structure.

## 3. Creature identity
Preserve:
- fish/aquatic-derived mythic face rather than a normal human face;
- coral/red identity;
- visible fin/gill structures;
- scale or aquatic skin cues in large readable masses;
- water-linked silhouette language.

Avoid:
- literal fish body;
- generic human mage wearing blue;
- overly delicate mermaid/princess read;
- fin density that turns into noise at mobile scale.

## 4. Face / head direction
The head should read aquatic before costume:
- short-to-medium fish-derived facial structure;
- visible gill/cheek treatment;
- fin-like ear/head structures or a compact dorsal/temple-fin cue;
- large expressive eyes suitable for support acting;
- avoid a standard human face with decorative scales pasted on.

Expression:
**composed / attentive / restorative**, not aggressive.

## 5. Silhouette
Primary silhouette:
- slimmer and more vertical than 猼訑/狌狌;
- softer/open upper-body posture;
- lower body visually light enough to support hovering;
- one or two clear fin shapes separated from the torso;
- compact support focus near the hands/body;
- no large fan of trailing appendages that collides with 九尾狐.

At battle size the first read should be:
**red aquatic hover-support caster**.

## 6. Support focus / equipment — recommended direction
Preferred current concept direction:
**floating healing-water orb + compact ring focus/bracer**.

Why:
- immediately communicates water/support without generic staff-mage silhouette;
- keeps hands free for expressive cast/action animation;
- gives future VFX a clear source/attachment point;
- works naturally with hover locomotion;
- stays visually separate from 九尾狐's offensive catalyst/tail fan.

The orb/focus is a concept element only. It must not become a permanent gameplay hitbox or independent actor.

Avoid for the first concept:
- full-length staff;
- giant vessel;
- oversized floating machinery;
- many bottles/charms;
- weapon-first silhouette.

## 7. Color direction
- coral / warm red as creature identity;
- pearl / cream neutral;
- pale aqua / teal for water/support energy;
- limited dark neutral for separation;
- small metallic accent only if needed.

Do not recolor the whole body blue simply because the character uses water.

## 8. Concept pose
Use a 3/4 rear-support presentation:
- body floating slightly above the implied ground;
- torso upright/open;
- one hand guiding the water orb/focus;
- other hand ready for support casting;
- fins/gills clearly visible;
- no attack lunge;
- no extreme spell explosion;
- no VFX-heavy composition that hides the character.

## 9. Battle identity priority
Preserve in this order:
1. aquatic/fish-derived head + gill/fin read;
2. hover-support humanoid silhouette;
3. coral/red creature color mass;
4. compact water-orb/ring support focus;
5. one or two broad fin/scale accents;
6. clothing/material details;
7. decoration.

When simplifying, remove from 7 upward.

## 10. Future locomotion gate — CLOSED FOR NOW
After static runtime approval, future movement should use the shared **Aquatic Hover Humanoid** archetype:
- suspended baseline;
- body drift rather than footstep cycle;
- fins / cloth / tail or lower-body accents follow travel;
- modest pitch/lean toward movement direction;
- no fake walking;
- no gameplay-coordinate oscillation.

This is not authorized for asset generation yet.

## 11. Future action + VFX gate — CLOSED FOR NOW
When later authorized:
- Basic/Heavy/Special/Awakening each receive a short action/cast micro-animation as needed;
- matching VFX timing is designed with the action, not separately;
- support/healing actions should visibly originate from the approved water focus/hand language;
- VFX must not change authoritative combat range, AoE, heal logic or timing.

## 12. Current acceptance gate
Approve the Concept only if:
- it unmistakably reads aquatic/fish-derived rather than human mage;
- it reads Support rather than attacker;
- the hover silhouette feels intentional;
- the water focus is compact and readable;
- silhouette stays distinct from 九尾狐;
- the design remains compatible with future shared hover locomotion and cast hooks.

**Next exact action:** generate/review one 赤鱬 Concept image from this spec only. No later-gate assets in the same image.
