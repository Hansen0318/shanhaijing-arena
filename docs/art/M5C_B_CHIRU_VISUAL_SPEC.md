# M5C-B Batch1 Visual Spec — 赤鱬

## Status
**CONCEPT DIRECTION — PLAYER REVIEW PENDING**

## 1. Role
Type: Blast
Role: Support
Combat identity: ranged healer / rear support.

## 2. Body plan
Humanoid / anthropomorphic biped with aquatic traits. Slim to medium build, softer silhouette than attackers, clear arm/hand articulation for support casting.

## 3. Creature identity
Preserve:
- aquatic/fish traits;
- red coloration as a defining cue;
- fin/gill/scale elements;
- water-linked visual language.

Avoid:
- literal fish body;
- mermaid tail that prevents shared ground locomotion;
- generic human mage with only blue clothing.

## 4. Equipment language
Healing vessel, water orb, ring staff, shell-like focus, floating flask, or compact support device are valid. Blast identity may be expressed through water-pressure/projectile apparatus rather than explosives.

## 5. Color direction
Red/coral primary accents, pale aqua/teal water effects, pearl/cream neutral, limited metallic trim.

## 6. portraitSquare
Head + upper torso. Fin/gill identity visible. Expression composed and attentive.

## 7. collectionArt
Rear-support pose with water focus/orb, body turned away from frontline. Elegant but practical.

## 8. battleIdle
Humanoid ranged-support stance; one hand/device ready, torso slightly back from engagement.

## 9. idle micro-animation
4-frame loop:
- gentle breathing;
- fin/hair/cloth sway;
- tiny water-orb pulse or float;
- small posture reset;
- no actor displacement.

## 10. Batch2 direction
Hit: sharp flinch with water effect disruption.
KO: lowered/weak posture.
Cast: concise hand/device lift, then return to idle.

## 11. Acceptance
Must read as aquatic Support, not melee fighter; support prop and aquatic traits remain readable at mobile scale.
