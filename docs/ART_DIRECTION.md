# Art Direction v1

## Presentation
- Mobile landscape.
- 2.5D / three-quarter arena perspective.
- Strong silhouettes and readable VFX at phone size.
- Combat clarity has priority over fine detail.

## Character style
Anthropomorphic Shanhaijing heroes:
- primarily humanoid combat readability;
- preserve defining beast/serpent/insect/aquatic/flying traits;
- designs should feel like playable heroes, not generic humans in themed costumes.

## Animation strategy
Prototype: placeholders and minimal motion.
Target production approach can combine 2D sprite/cutout/skeletal motion with VFX.

Minimum target animation states for a production-ready character:
- Idle
- Move
- Basic
- Heavy
- Special
- Awakening
- Hit
- Death

Flying characters may remain normally airborne instead of requiring full takeoff/landing cycles in every move.

## Directional art
Do not commit to eight-direction full animation in the prototype.
Prefer a fixed three-quarter presentation with limited facing variants/mirroring until playtesting proves more directions are needed.
